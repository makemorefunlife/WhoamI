# Core Product Principles

The following principles are considered foundational.

---

# 1. Narrative Over Dashboard

## Evidence

Humans process stories more deeply than disconnected facts.

Research consistently shows that narrative structure increases engagement, memory, and persuasion while reducing resistance.

(Narrative Transportation)

---

## Product Rule

Never present Ahaitsme as a collection of scores.

Instead:

```
Facts
↓

Interpretation

↓

Story

↓

Reflection
```

The report should feel like reading an insightful biography rather than inspecting analytics.

---

# 2. Insight Before Evidence

## Evidence

Users abandon experiences when cognitive load is too high.

(Progressive Disclosure)

---

## Product Rule

Always reveal:

**Meaning first**

before

**Explanation**

Example

Good

> You recharge by having space to think before responding.

Then

> Why?

Then

> Deterministic evidence

Bad

```
Element Balance

↓

Stem Interaction

↓

Five Pages

↓

Conclusion
```

---

# 3. Personal Before General

## Evidence

People remember information significantly better when it relates directly to themselves.

(Self-Reference Effect)

---

## Product Rule

Avoid writing about personality systems.

Always write about the person.

Instead of

> Wood energy often behaves...

Write

> In your life this usually appears as...

---

# 4. Human Interpretation, Not Fortune Telling

## Evidence

People distrust AI that sounds mystical.

People trust AI that explains its reasoning.

(Algorithm Appreciation)

---

## Product Rule

Every important conclusion should be traceable.

```
Deterministic Fact

+

Deterministic Fact

↓

Interpretation

↓

Narrative
```

The AI is an interpreter.

Never a psychic.

---

# 5. Emotional Safety

## Evidence

Absolute statements trigger defensive rejection.

(Psychological Reactance)

---

## Product Rule

Avoid

"You are..."

Prefer

"You may notice..."

"This combination often leads to..."

"There is a tendency toward..."

The goal is understanding.

Not diagnosis.

---

# 6. Peak Moments Matter

## Evidence

People remember experiences primarily by

- the strongest emotional moment
- the ending

(Peak-End Rule)

---

## Product Rule

The report does NOT need to be equally exciting throughout.

Instead

Design

- 2–3 memorable insights

and

One beautiful ending.

---

# 7. Visual Simplicity Creates Trust

## Evidence

People unconsciously perceive information as more truthful when it is easier to process.

(Processing Fluency)

---

## Product Rule

Prioritize

- whitespace
- typography
- pacing
- visual hierarchy

Beauty is not decoration.

Beauty improves credibility.

---

A premium report should guide the reader through an emotional journey.

---

## Phase 1

Feeling

Goal

---

## Phase 2

Curiosity

Feeling

Goal

Increase engagement.

---

## Phase 3

Discovery

Feeling

> "That's surprisingly true."

Goal

Deliver meaningful insight.

---

## Phase 4

Integration

Feeling

> "Now it all makes sense."

Goal

Connect individual observations into a coherent story.

---

## Phase 5

Reflection

Feeling

> "I understand myself better."

Goal

Leave the reader feeling hopeful rather than judged.

# Information Pacing

Long reports fail because they exhaust attention.

Our pacing principles:

- Short paragraphs
- Frequent visual breaks
- Consistent hierarchy
- One major idea at a time
- Conclusion before explanation
- Alternate narrative with visuals

Every few hundred words the presentation style should change.

Examples

Narrative

↓

Visualization

↓

Comparison

↓

Reflection

↓

Narrative

---

# Relationship Reports

Relationship analysis is especially vulnerable to defensiveness.

Therefore:

Never present

Person A vs Person B

Instead present

```
Person A

+

Person B

↓

The Relationship
```

The relationship becomes the subject.

Not either individual.

---

# Comparison Principles

Good

> One creates structure.
>
> One creates momentum.

Bad

> One is organized.
>
> One is chaotic.

Good

> Different operating systems.

Bad

> Correct vs incorrect.

---

# Match Scores

Research strongly discourages reducing complex relationships to percentages.

Therefore

Ahaitsme should avoid

- compatibility %
- love %
- friendship %

Instead communicate

- strengths
- tensions
- dynamics
- growth opportunities

---

# Memory Design

Users will not remember everything.

Therefore we intentionally design memorable moments.

Target

- 2–3 major "Aha" insights
- One memorable ending
- One shareable takeaway

Everything else supports those moments.

---

# Sharing Principles

Private insight

↓

Creates trust

Public summary

↓

Creates sharing

Not everything valuable should be shareable.

Not everything shareable should be deeply personal.

# Design Principles

| Principle | Product Rule |
|------------|--------------|
| Story before dashboard | People understand narratives better than isolated facts. |
| Insight before evidence | Meaning first, proof second. |
| Personal before general | Always relate concepts back to the individual. |
| Interpretation before diagnosis | Explain tendencies without defining identity. |
| Simplicity creates trust | Clean design increases perceived credibility. |
| Emotional safety | Never make the reader feel judged. |
| Progressive disclosure | Reveal complexity only when requested. |
| Relationship over score | Explain dynamics rather than calculating percentages. |
| Reflection over prediction | Focus on understanding rather than forecasting. |
| Peak moments | Design memorable insights and endings. |

# Final Philosophy

Ahaitsme should never feel like reading data.

It should never feel like taking a test.

It should never feel like receiving a diagnosis.

It should feel like sitting with someone who has patiently studied your life, understands how all the pieces connect, and explains them with warmth, clarity, honesty, and respect.

Deterministic systems provide the facts.

AI provides understanding.

The product transforms understanding into reflection.

That transformation is the experience Ahaitsme exists to create.
