## 1. Purpose

Relationship reports follow the company mission in `01_Product_Vision.md`.

This document defines how that mission shows up in **relationship UX**: a premium reading experience driven by deterministic facts and thoughtful interpretation—without becoming a dating app, personality test, or diagnostic dashboard.

The goal is not to overwhelm users with information.

The goal is to create repeated moments of clarity that support better-informed judgment—never to decide for the user.

Assessment constitution governing that boundary is owned by `04_Assessment_Philosophy.md`. This Bible applies it to relationship reading UX.

---

# 2. Core Philosophy

Every relationship report follows the same architecture.

```text
Fact (Deterministic SSOT)

↓

Meaning (Holistic AI Interpretation)

↓

Experience (Beautiful UX)
```

### Fact

Facts come only from deterministic systems.

Examples:

* Saju calculations
* Psychological axes
* Canonical projections
* Verified relationship signals

Facts are authoritative.

Typed client-visible classifications follow decision `018`—not LLM prose.

---

### Meaning

AI never invents facts.

AI reasons over relationships between facts.

Good interpretation explains how multiple deterministic signals interact.

Bad interpretation explains one signal in isolation.

How the narrator speaks—and **Recognition before Advice**—are owned by the Narrative Style Bible, not this document.

---

### Experience

The interface exists to communicate understanding.

Not to expose computation.

Users should feel they are reading a carefully crafted story rather than inspecting a diagnostic dashboard.

# 3. UX Principles

## Story before Data

Never present information as disconnected metrics.

Every section should answer:

> What does this mean?

before

> Why?

### Meaning before detail

A score, axis, signal, or trait has no experience value until the reader understands what it means in real life.

The report should introduce the human meaning first, then provide the detail needed to support it.

### Story before inventory

The report must not expose every available insight simply because the system can produce it.

Content belongs only when it advances the reader’s realization journey.

A smaller number of connected insights is more valuable than a large inventory of unrelated truths.

### Story and decision relevance

**Story leads the reading experience; decision relevance determines what deserves attention.**

Story-before-data shapes *how* people read.

Decision relevance shapes *what* earns a place on the path.

Neither replaces the other: we do not lead with raw decision dashboards, and we do not tell beautiful stories about things that do not matter to the relationship.

---

## Compare before Explain

Whenever two people are involved,

show the relationship first.

Explain afterwards.

Humans understand contrast faster than isolated traits.

---

## One Insight per Screen

Every screen should communicate one memorable idea.

Never compete for attention.

---

## Progressive Disclosure

The default path should remain simple.

Depth is optional.

Structure:

```text
Headline

↓

Short explanation

↓

Evidence

↓

Deep Read
```

Users should never be forced to consume expert-level detail.

---

## Relationship as Subject

The subject of a relationship report is the **relationship**—the living pattern between two people—not a contest of who is right.

Prefer dynamics, strengths, and tensions over A-vs-B score warfare.

---

## Rhythmic Reading

A 15–30 minute report should feel like a guided journey.

Alternate between:

* Narrative
* Visual comparison
* Summary
* Illustration
* Reflection

Avoid long uninterrupted blocks of text. This "rhythmic interruption" pattern helps sustain engagement in long-form reading experiences.

---

## Visual Reset

Readers need breathing room.

Between heavy sections provide:

* comparison cards
* summary cards
* chapter breaks
* signature moments

not more paragraphs.

# 4. Truthfulness

Constitutional assessment limits (evidence before interpretation, uncertainty over false certainty, confidence vs truth) are owned by `04_Assessment_Philosophy.md`.

This section states the UX-facing application of those laws.

Never invent.

Never exaggerate.

Never imply unsupported precision.

Unknown is better than fake certainty.

---

## Evidence First

Confidence should influence tone.

Confidence should never replace evidence.

(See `04_Assessment_Philosophy.md` Law 5.)

Why we believe these trust patterns → `03_Market_Research.md`.

---

## No Fake Scores

Only display scores when they are deterministic.

Never create cosmetic numbers for aesthetics.

(Internal validity does not require user visibility — `04_Assessment_Philosophy.md` Law 8.)

---

## Explain Limits

If something cannot honestly be inferred,

say so.

---

# 5. AI Interpretation Rules (UX contract)

AI is not a calculator.

AI is an interpreter of deterministic facts.

The model should synthesize multiple facts into one coherent meaning—not enumerate isolated signals.

Avoid:

> You have Peach Blossom.

Prefer synthesis of how signals interact within the full relationship context.

Voice, genre bans, and **Recognition before Advice** → Narrative Style Bible.  
What may appear as typed chips/badges → decision `018`.

---

# 6. Emotional Design

Users should feel:

Curious

↓

Understood

↓

Validated

↓

Encouraged

Never judged.

Never manipulated.

Never frightened.

---

# 7. Premium Reading

Premium does not mean:

* longer
* denser
* more technical

Premium means:

* better sequencing
* stronger storytelling
* thoughtful pacing
* cleaner visuals
* more meaningful insights

---

# 8. Information Hierarchy

Priority order:

1. What matters most
2. Why it matters
3. Evidence
4. Nuance
5. Technical detail

Never reverse this order.

“What matters most” is filtered by **decision relevance**; how it is delivered remains **story-led**.

---

# 9. Platform Consistency

These principles apply equally to:

* Romantic
* Friend
* Work
* Family
* Cohabitation

Only the deterministic facts change.

The reading experience remains consistent.

Domain-specific module maps belong in domain blueprints—not here.

---

# 10. Non-goals

The product is not intended to become:

* a social network
* a habit tracker
* a therapy replacement
* a psychology textbook
* an astrology encyclopedia
* a diagnostic tool
* a decision-maker that replaces the user’s judgment

It is a premium relationship reading experience that supports clearer decisions.
