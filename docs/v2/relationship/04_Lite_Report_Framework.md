# 04_Lite_Report_Framework.md

## Purpose

Define the structure of the free (Lite) relationship report.

The goal of the Lite report is to:

* create curiosity
* provide meaningful insight
* deliver immediate value
* encourage deeper exploration

The Lite report should be simple, fast, and emotionally relatable.

---

# Report Structure

```text
Compatibility Score
↓

Why You Click
↓

Potential Friction

↓

Communication Tip

↓

One-Line Summary

↓

Premium Teaser
```

---

# Section 1

Compatibility Score

## Purpose

Provide a simple overall compatibility indicator.

## Output

```json
{
  "compatibility_score": 82
}
```

## User Display

```text
82 / 100

Strong Compatibility
```

---

# Section 2

Why You Click

## Purpose

Explain why the relationship feels natural.

## Input Sources

* Shared behavior tags
* Complementary strengths
* Similar motivations

## Example Output

```text
Both of you value growth and self-improvement.

You tend to respect each other's independence.

You are both comfortable pursuing meaningful goals.
```

## Rules

Focus on:

* connection
* strengths
* mutual understanding

Avoid:

* conflict
* criticism
* warnings

````

---

# Section 3

Potential Friction

## Purpose

Highlight the most likely source of misunderstanding.

## Input Sources

Relationship Rules Engine

Examples:

- planning_vs_spontaneity
- direct_vs_avoidant_conflict
- emotional_connection_vs_distance

## Example Output

```text
One of you prefers planning ahead.

The other prefers flexibility.

This may create different expectations around schedules and commitments.
````

## Rules

Focus on:

* behavior

Avoid:

* blame
* fault
* judgment

````

---

# Section 4

Communication Tip

## Purpose

Provide one practical recommendation.

## Example Output

```text
Discuss expectations before important plans.

Small alignment conversations can prevent larger misunderstandings.
````

## Rules

Tips must be:

* actionable
* simple
* realistic

Maximum:

* 2 short paragraphs

````

---

# Section 5

One-Line Summary

## Purpose

Provide a memorable takeaway.

## Example Output

```text
Strong connection with occasional planning-related tension.
````

Alternative Examples:

```text
Different communication styles, shared long-term goals.

Emotionally different, but highly complementary.

Natural chemistry supported by mutual respect.
```

---

# Section 6

Premium Teaser

## Purpose

Create curiosity without hiding core value.

Users should already feel satisfied with the Lite report.

Premium should feel deeper, not necessary.

## Example Output

```text
Want to understand:

- how conflict develops between you?
- what each person secretly needs in relationships?
- where long-term tension may appear?
- how your innate tendencies compare?

Unlock the full compatibility report.
```

---

# Report Length

Target:

```text
300 - 600 words
```

Maximum:

```text
800 words
```

---

# Tone Guidelines

Use:

* conversational language
* behavioral language
* relatable examples

Avoid:

* personality labels
* psychological jargon
* deterministic statements
* fortune-telling language

````

Examples:

Good:

```text
You may have different expectations around planning.
````

Bad:

```text
You are fundamentally incompatible.
```

---

# Important Principle

Lite reports should answer:

```text
Why do we naturally connect?

Where could we misunderstand each other?

What can we do about it?
```

The report should feel useful even without purchasing Premium.
