# 03_Relationship_Behavior_Rules.md

## Purpose

Convert behavior tag combinations into real-world relationship patterns.

This framework should focus on:

* misunderstandings
* friction points
* complementary strengths
* communication styles
* growth opportunities

Do NOT generate personality labels.

Focus on observable relationship behaviors.

---

# Rule Structure

```yaml
rule_id:

trigger:

relationship_effect:

common_thoughts:

strengths:

growth_tip:
```

---

# Planning vs Spontaneity

```yaml
rule_id: planning_vs_spontaneity

trigger:

  user_a:
    planning: high

  user_b:
    spontaneity: high

relationship_effect:

  - schedule_conflict
  - expectation_mismatch

common_thoughts:

  user_a:
    - "Why didn't you tell me earlier?"

  user_b:
    - "Why does everything need a plan?"

strengths:

  - balance_between_structure_and_flexibility

growth_tip:

  - align expectations before important events
```

---

# Emotional Connection vs Emotional Distance

```yaml
rule_id: emotional_connection_vs_distance

trigger:

  user_a:
    emotional_connection: high

  user_b:
    emotional_distance: high

relationship_effect:

  - intimacy_gap
  - communication_frequency_difference

common_thoughts:

  user_a:
    - "Why are they pulling away?"

  user_b:
    - "I just need some space."

strengths:

  - emotional balance

growth_tip:

  - discuss preferred communication frequency
```
