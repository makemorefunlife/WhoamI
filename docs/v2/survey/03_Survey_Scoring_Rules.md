# 03_Survey_Scoring_Rules.md

## Purpose

Define how survey answers are translated into Human Framework scores.

This document is the official scoring source for the Survey System.

---

# Scoring Rules

* Q1 ~ Q9 are scored.
* Q10 is not scored.
* Q5 uses Weight = 2.
* All other questions use Weight = 1.
* Primary Axes are calculated from Secondary Axes.
* Multiple axes may increase simultaneously.
* No forced balancing.
* No axis cancels another axis.
