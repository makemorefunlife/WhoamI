# 01_Survey_System

## Purpose

Generate the Current Self Profile.

The Survey System measures the user's current behavioral patterns, decision tendencies, relationship tendencies, stress response, and recovery patterns.

The Survey System does not measure Innate Self.

Innate Self is generated separately by the Saju System.

---

# Human Framework Dependency

# Design Principles

* Scenario-based questions
* No personality labels
* No right answers
* Measure behavior rather than identity
* Completion time under 2 minutes
* Focus on current state rather than innate traits
