# 11_Comparison_Framework

## Purpose

Define how Current Self and Innate Self are compared within Ahaitsme.

The goal is not prediction.

The goal is understanding alignment, tension, and growth opportunities.

---

# Core Concepts

## Alignment

Current Self and Innate Self show similar tendencies.

This area usually feels natural and sustainable.

---

## Suppression

Innate Self is naturally strong in an area but Current Self shows a lower expression.

This often indicates environmental pressure, fear, or adaptation.

---

## Overadaptation

Current Self is significantly higher than Innate Self.

This often reflects learned behavior, social expectations, or survival strategies.

---

## Energy Leakage

A large gap exists between Current Self and Innate Self.

Maintaining this gap requires continuous effort and may create fatigue.

---

## Growth Opportunity

Innate strengths exist but are not yet fully utilized.

This area may represent future development potential.

---

# Gap Severity

Gap Severity represents the magnitude of difference between Current Self and Innate Self.

The exact scoring thresholds may evolve over time.

Conceptually:

## Low Gap

Current Self and Innate Self are closely aligned.

---

## Moderate Gap

Noticeable differences exist but can be maintained without significant strain.

---

## High Gap

Large differences exist.

Maintaining this gap may require substantial effort and can contribute to fatigue, frustration, or reduced well-being.

---

# Analysis Outputs

The comparison engine should identify:

* Areas of Alignment
* Areas of Suppression
* Areas of Overadaptation
* Sources of Energy Leakage
* Growth Opportunities
* Causes of Decision Fatigue
* Causes of Relationship Friction
* Sustainable Development Paths
