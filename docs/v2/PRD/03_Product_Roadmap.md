## Product Evolution

```text
Self Understanding → Decision Intelligence → Relationship Intelligence → Life Intelligence Platform
```

---

## Phase 1: MVP

**Goal:** Validate Current vs Innate vs Gap → paid Deep report.

**Status:** In development.

**Includes:** Landing, auth, survey, saju lite, gap rules + preview, lite/deep prompts, payment, basic dashboard.

**Engines:** Survey, Saju (lite), Gap, Pattern, Narrative, Report, UI (partial runtime).

---

## Phase 2: Relationship Intelligence

**Goal:** Compatibility, communication, decision style between two people.

**Features:** Manual analysis, invite flow, public profile, deep compatibility report, team/business variants (later).

**New engine:** Relationship Engine — `docs/relationship/` (draft docs exist).

---

## Phase 3: Decision Intelligence

**Goal:** Better decisions over time from real choices.

**Features:** Decision coaching, journal, reflection tracking, pattern learning, personal decision DB, personalized guidance.

**New engine:** Decision Engine.

---

## Phase 4: Continuous Personal Growth

**Goal:** Longitudinal growth platform.

**Features:** Weekly/monthly insights, habit reflection, emotional trends, life pattern timeline.

**New engine:** Growth Engine.

---

## Phase 5: Social & Organization

**Goal:** Groups beyond individuals.

**Features:** Couple/family/team analysis, leadership & hiring fit, org dashboard.

**New engines:** Team Engine, Organization Engine.

---

## Phase 6: AI Life Companion

**Goal:** Continuous, context-aware self-understanding.

**Features:** Persistent memory, proactive suggestions, long-horizon coaching.

**New engine:** Life Intelligence Engine.

---

## Success Milestones

| Milestone | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| MVP | Users complete free loop and understand Current vs Innate |
| Relationship | Users gain actionable insight about another person |
| Decision | Users report better outcomes from coached decisions |
| Growth | Sustained engagement over months |
| Platform | Default OS for personal understanding + decisions |

---

## Long-term Vision

Not another horoscope or one-off personality test.  
An AI platform that accumulates **understanding, relationships, and decision history** to support clearer life choices — with evidence-based, non-deterministic guidance.
