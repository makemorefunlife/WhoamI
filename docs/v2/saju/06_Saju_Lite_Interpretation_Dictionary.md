# 06_Saju_Lite_Interpretation_Dictionary.md

## Purpose

This document defines the interpretation dictionary used by Fast Saju Analysis.

The purpose of this document is to generate simple, user-friendly descriptions of the user's Innate Self.

This document should not expose traditional Saju terminology directly to users.

Users should receive human-centered insights, not technical Saju explanations.

---

# Design Philosophy

Internal Engine

```text
Day Master

Day Branch
```

↓

User Facing Insight

```text
Who am I?

How do I relate to people?

What kind of environment suits me?
```

---

# Exposure Rules

Do NOT expose:

```text
Day Master

Day Branch

Ten Gods

Five Elements

Yuan Zhen

Growth Stage

Special Stars
```

---

Instead translate into:

```text
Personality

Relationship Style

Energy Pattern

Behavioral Tendencies
```

---

# Lite Analysis Structure

Fast Saju Analysis contains:

```text
1. Human Framework Radar

2. Core Personality Insight

3. Relationship Tendency Insight

4. One Line Summary
```

---

# Core Personality Insight

Generated primarily from:

```text
Day Master
```

Purpose:

```text
Who am I?

How do I naturally operate?

What motivates me?
```

Output Style:

```text
Natural language only.

No Saju terminology.
```

Example

```text
You tend to be curious about people and the world around you.

You enjoy discovering new perspectives and often prefer freedom when making decisions.

While you appear adaptable, there are parts of yourself that you reveal only to people you truly trust.
```

---

# Relationship Tendency Insight

Generated primarily from:

```text
Day Branch
```

Purpose:

```text
How do I build relationships?

What makes me feel comfortable?

What kind of connection do I naturally seek?
```

Output Style:

```text
Natural language only.

No Saju terminology.
```

Example

```text
You tend to value trust and emotional sincerity in relationships.

You generally prefer relationships that feel natural rather than forced.

When you feel respected and understood, you become much more open and engaged.
```

---

# One Line Summary

Purpose:

Provide a memorable summary of the user's Innate Self.

Generated from:

```text
Day Master

+

Day Branch

+

Human Framework Profile
```

Output Examples

```text
A naturally curious person who values freedom and authentic connection.
```

```text
Someone who grows through new experiences while seeking stability in close relationships.
```

```text
A thoughtful observer who values independence but forms deep bonds with trusted people.
```

# Design Principles

1. Use human-centered language.
2. Avoid traditional Saju terminology.
3. Focus on clarity and relatability.
4. Keep insights concise.
5. Do not include deep interpretation.
6. Create curiosity for deeper analysis.
7. Describe the person, not the chart.
8. Prioritize self-understanding over fortune telling.
