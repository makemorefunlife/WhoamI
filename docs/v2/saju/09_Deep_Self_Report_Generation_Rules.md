# 09_Deep_Self_Report_Generation_Rules.md

## Purpose

This document defines the Narrative Engine of Ahaitsme.

The purpose of this engine is not to generate personality descriptions.

The purpose is to generate meaningful self-understanding narratives.

This layer transforms:

```text
Deep Interpretation Signals

+

Human Meaning Signals

+

Current Self Profile
```

into:

```text
Deep Self Report
```

Users should feel:

> "This explains my life."

Not:

> "This explains my chart."

# Core Philosophy

The report should explain:

* Identity
* Behaviors
* Emotional Experiences
* Repeating Patterns
* Growth Opportunities

The report should NOT explain:

* Saju Structure
* Ten Gods
* Five Elements
* Technical Astrology Concepts
* Calculation Logic

The user is not trying to understand Saju.

The user is trying to understand themselves.

# Narrative Flow Structure

Every major narrative should follow:

```text
Observation

↓

Meaning

↓

Consequence

↓

Growth Direction
```

Example:

Observation:

The user often handles things alone.

↓

Meaning:

Receiving help may feel uncomfortable.

↓

Consequence:

Responsibility accumulates.

↓

Growth Direction:

Learn trust-based collaboration.

````

# Narrative Style Rules

## Rule 1

Speak directly to the user.

Preferred:

```text
You often...
````

Avoid:

```text
This chart suggests...
```

---

## Rule 2

Explain experiences.

Avoid labels.

Avoid:

```text
You are highly independent.
```

Prefer:

```text
You may feel uncomfortable relying on others,
even when support is available.
```

---

## Rule 3

Explain the pattern beneath the behavior.

Avoid:

```text
You overthink.
```

Prefer:

```text
You may spend extra time thinking because making the wrong choice feels costly.
```

---

## Rule 4

Focus on recognition.

The goal is:

```text
That is me.
```

Not:

```text
Interesting information.
```

# Emotional Resonance Rules

Strong narratives often include:

* Hidden Motivations
* Hidden Fears
* Hidden Tensions
* Repeating Life Patterns

Example:

```text
You are not avoiding help.

You may be avoiding the feeling that comes with needing help.
```

---

Example:

```text
People may see you as strong.

But strength may have become a role you learned to play for a long time.
```

# Growth Narrative Framework

Growth guidance should follow:

```text
Awareness

↓

Experiment

↓

Practice
```

Example:

Awareness:

You may equate asking for help with losing independence.

↓

Experiment:

Ask for support in one low-risk situation.

↓

Practice:

Observe whether receiving support actually reduces your autonomy.

---

# Report Quality Rules

A report should:

✓ Feel personal

✓ Feel emotionally accurate

✓ Explain patterns

✓ Create recognition

✓ Offer growth direction

---

A report should NOT:

✗ Predict the future

✗ Use fear-based language

✗ Use technical Saju terminology

✗ Sound like a personality test

✗ Sound like fortune telling

---

# Golden Rule

Never explain the chart.

Always explain the person.

The report succeeds when the user feels:

> "This is my story."

Not:

> "This is my Saju."
