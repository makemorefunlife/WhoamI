# 08_Self_Analysis_Signal_Mapping.md

## Purpose

Transform Saju-derived signals into meaningful human insights.

This layer exists to bridge the gap between:

```text
Saju Data

↓

Human Meaning

↓

Behavior

↓

Life Pattern

↓

Emotional Experience
```

The goal is NOT to explain Saju.

The goal IS to explain the user's life.

Users should never feel:

> "This describes my chart."

Users should feel:

> "This describes my life."

---

# Core Philosophy

Avoid:

```text
Signal
↓
Trait
```

Example:

"Strong Companion Star → Independent"

This is shallow.

---

Use:

```text
Signal
↓
Human Signal
↓
Identity
↓
Core Need
↓
Core Fear
↓
Core Wound
↓
Behavior Pattern
↓
Repeating Life Pattern
↓
Shadow Pattern
↓
Common Trigger
↓
Emotional Theme
↓
Narrative Insight
↓
Growth Direction
```

---

# Identity Layer

Purpose:

Describe how the person unconsciously sees themselves.

Format:

```text
I am the kind of person who...
```

Examples:

```text
I am someone who should handle things myself.
```

```text
I am someone who must remain reliable.
```

```text
I am someone who values freedom over comfort.
```

```text
I am someone who needs to understand before acting.
```

Identity statements create recognition and emotional attachment.

# Narrative Insight Layer

Purpose:

Generate emotionally resonant life interpretations.

Format:

Second-person narrative.

Always:

```text
You...
```

Never:

```text
This chart...
```

Never:

```text
Because of strong resource stars...
```

Good Example:

You are not someone who lacks support.

You are someone who often feels that accepting support comes with a loss of independence.

As a result, you carry more than you need to.

Over time, this creates a cycle where people see you as strong, while you quietly become exhausted.

Narrative Insights should explain life patterns, not astrology.

# Suggested Action Framework

Every Growth Direction should include:

## Awareness

Recognition of current pattern.

Example:

```text
You are not avoiding help.

You are avoiding the feeling that comes with needing help.
```

---

## Experiment

Small behavioral test.

Example:

```text
Ask for help with one low-risk task this week.
```

---

## Practice

Long-term growth behavior.

Example:

```text
Observe whether receiving support actually reduces your value, or only challenges an old belief.
```

```text

Patterns

Emotions

```

The user is not paying to understand Saju.

The user is paying to understand themselves.
