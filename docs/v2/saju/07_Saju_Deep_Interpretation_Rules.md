# 07_Saju_Deep_Interpretation_Rules.md

## Purpose

This document defines the Deep Self Analysis Signal Engine.

The purpose of this engine is not to explain Saju.

The purpose is to identify deep interpretation signals that help explain the person.

This document transforms:

```text
Extended NormalizedSaju

+

InnateSelfProfile

+

CurrentSelfProfile
```

into Deep Interpretation Signals.

This document does not generate final user-facing narratives.

Final human meaning, identity narratives, emotional themes, and report content are generated by downstream documents:

```text
08_Self_Analysis_Signal_Mapping.md

09_Deep_Self_Report_Generation_Rules.md

10_Deep_Self_Report_UI_Patterns.md
```

# Core Questions

The engine attempts to answer at the signal level:

* Who am I originally?
* Why am I living this way now?
* Why does life sometimes feel difficult?
* Why do I repeat similar relationships?
* Why do I make similar decisions?
* What kind of environment helps me thrive?
* What direction helps me become more aligned with myself?

These questions should be answered as interpretation signals, not final user-facing narrative.

# Interpretation Principles

1. Explain the person, not the chart.
2. Use everyday language.
3. Avoid technical Saju terminology.
4. Focus on behavioral patterns.
5. Focus on self-understanding over prediction.
6. Explain "why" behind patterns.
7. Translate structures into real-life examples.
8. Prioritize alignment over fortune telling.
9. Do not make deterministic predictions.
10. Always connect insights back to lived experience.
11. Generate signals before narratives. Final narratives belong to the Human Meaning Engine and Narrative Engine.
